FAQ
1.
How do I know my guestlist is received?
Once you add a name, it appears in "My Entries" immediately. This means it's received and registered. You don't need to do anything else. When you put a guestlist entry the admin receives directly a notification on the phone, so don't worry we don't miss it.
2.
What does "Confirmed" mean on my guestlist?
Confirmed means the club has received and approved the entry. Your guest is on the official list.
3.
Why can't I modify a guestlist entry after it's confirmed?
Once the club has confirmed the entry, it's been sent to them. Changes after that? Cancel your current entry and put a new one.
4.
My entry isn't confirmed yet — should I worry?
Not necessarily. Entries are confirmed by the club or it's not available/open yet in Fourvenues. As long as the entry shows in your list, it's registered.
5.
Why does it say "Guestlist not available"?
This means the guestlist for that event isn't open yet. Check back closer to the date.
6.
Does "Guestlist not available" mean there won't be a guestlist?
Yes & no. It means it hasn't been activated yet. It is possible that it can open at any time — keep checking.
7.
Can I cancel an entry?
Yes, tap the ✕ button on the entry in "My Entries."
8.
My guest will not make it — what do I do?
Cancel the entry in the app as soon as possible, especially if it was already confirmed.
9.
The app isn't loading — what do I do?
Pull down to refresh. If that doesn't work, log out and log back in or close the app and open again. Make sure you have a stable internet connection.
